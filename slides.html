The problem with browser tuners

The needle never sits still.

You pluck a string and the pointer twitches across half the dial. You cannot tell whether you are sharp, flat, or done. So you turn the peg, guess, and turn it back.

That is not your ear. It is the software.

Why it happens

A quiet room is enough to break it.

Here is how far a typical browser tuner's needle jumps between one frame and the next, measured in cents.

Silent room0.02¢
Quiet room2.1¢
Noisy room10.1¢
Phone across the room12.3¢

Twelve cents per frame is the needle swinging thirteen degrees, sixty times a second.

What Align does instead

Four filters between the microphone and the needle.

Confidence gate. Frames the detector is not sure about are thrown away, not displayed.

Octave snapping. A ringing neighbour cannot drag the reading an octave off.

Outlier rejection. One wild frame is ignored. A run of them means you changed note.

Adaptive smoothing. Smooths hard while the pitch is steady, opens up the instant you turn a peg.

Accuracy
0.04 cents

across seventeen notes from C2 to E5.

A cent is a hundredth of a semitone. A trained ear picks out about five of them on a sustained note. Align resolves a hundred times finer than that, and it reads correctly even when the fundamental is missing entirely, which is what usually happens when a phone microphone is asked to hear a low E.

The other classic failure

Cheap tuners jump an octave.

Pluck a low E and half of them will confidently show you E3. It happens because they pick the strongest match in the signal, and on a plucked string that is often the wrong one.

Align takes the first strong match instead of the loudest.

When it lands

Green, and a little chime.

Inside three cents the needle turns green, the gauge fills, and you hear a short two note blip. You can keep your eyes on your hands instead of the screen.

It fires once. It will not chatter at you while the needle hovers on the boundary, and it will not repeat while the string holds. Tap the note in the header to mute it.

Strings you have finished stay marked, so you can see your progress along the row.

Nothing is sent anywhere

There is no server.

No account, no sign up, no tracking, no ads, no third party scripts. The audio is read from your microphone, analysed on your device, and discarded frame by frame. Nothing is recorded and nothing is uploaded.

The whole application is one HTML file. Your tunings and settings live in your own browser.

What you play is nobody else's data.
